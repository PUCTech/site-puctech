## Description

Minerva is an app that lets university students keep their academic life in one place: courses, grades, absences, assignments and files. The idea came from a common problem in college: how hard it is to plan your time.

It was built during First Steps, a PUC Tech program, with the routine of Brazilian university students in mind.

## Goal and scope

Each course is registered with its credits, workload and required average. Grades are entered with weights and the average is calculated on the spot. Absences are tracked and the app shows how many are left before the limit, calculated from the workload.

There's also a calendar for assignments, exams and deadlines, and a section for files.

## Results and lessons learned

The app is deployed in the cloud, with a Spring back end, a Streamlit interface and JWT authentication.
